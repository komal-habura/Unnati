Our Vision & Purpose

Rooted in the wisdom of the Bhagavad-gita, we aim to build a spiritually strong, emotionally resilient, and deeply connected sisterhood of youth girls.

Why This Community Started

Growing up in the modern world presents unique challenges. Social media pressure, career stress, and relationship anxieties often leave youth feeling lost or overwhelmed. The Unnati collective was created to be a sanctuary—a safe, nurturing space where young women can find inner peace, deep friendships, and undeniable strength.

We believe that applying timeless Vedic wisdom to modern life provides practical, lived solutions to today's problems. It is a space where ambition and devotion beautifully coexist.

Our Divine Inspiration

Everything we do is guided by the foundational text of yoga and devotion: Bhagavad-gita As It Is.

We humbly follow the guidance and profound teachings of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, Founder-Acharya of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON). His compassion, purity, and profound vision continue to transform hearts around the globe.

Core Vision for Youth Girls

✨

Spiritual Strength

Empowering girls to confidently face any challenge with resilience derived from daily spiritual practice, reflection, and prayer.

💖

Authentic Relationships

Fostering true sisterhood beyond surface-level interactions—building deep, lasting bonds based on shared spiritual values and emotional safety.
