Our Vision & Purpose

Rooted in the wisdom of the Bhagavad-gita, we aim to build a spiritually strong, emotionally resilient, and deeply connected sisterhood of youth girls.

Why This Community Started

To cultivate a dynamic community of spiritually rooted, emotionally strong, and purpose-driven young women who rise together — integrating devotion, self-mastery, leadership, and meaningful contribution for value-driven holistic growth.

Unnati is not just a youth club. It is a movement of conscious growth, a leadership incubator, and a living sisterhood that empowers women to flourish spiritually, personally, and socially.

His Divine Grace
A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada

Founder-Acharya of ISKCON

✦

Our journey is rooted in the teachings and example of a spiritual master who dedicated his life to sharing Krishna consciousness with the world. His life continues to inspire millions to live with purpose and devotion.

1896

The Beginning

Born as Abhay Charan De on September 1, 1896, in Calcutta, into a devout Vaishnava family.

1922

The Instruction

Met His Divine Grace Bhaktisiddanta Sarasvati Thakur, who requested him to preach in English.

1933

The Commitment

He became a dedicated disciple, and began publishing Back to Godhead, sharing Krishna consciousness in a simple, modern way.

1959

The Mission Expands

Traveled to the West at the age of 69 with nothing but faith and his books, to fulfill his spiritual master's order.

1966

The Legacy

Founded ISKCON in New York on July 13, 1966. His teachings continue through his books, disciples and institutions worldwide.
