The 5 Pillars of Unnati

Discover how each pillar supports spiritual growth, leadership, creativity, and sisterhood across our community.

Pillar Details

Click a pillar headline to jump directly to its full description and learn how it empowers young women through practice, creativity, and community.

01

Sakhi Circles

Weekly small-group mentorship spaces for emotional growth, spiritual reflection, and value-based discussions.

  • Format: Small groups (8-10 members) with a fixed mentor. Hybrid setting.
  • Discussions: Themes anchored in Bhagavad-gita covering emotional boundaries, identity, self-discipline, and handling modern pressures.
  • Safe Protocol: A confidential environment guided by trained mentors.
02

Study Pod

Weekday online reading circle studying Śrīla Prabhupāda’s books — building discipline, clarity, and spiritual depth.

  • Format: 20-minute daily sessions (Mon-Fri) on Zoom/Meet.
  • Structure: 19 minutes collective reading followed by reflection.
  • Culture: Habit & consistency over heavy lecturing. No pressure, just connection.
03

Creative Bhakti Lab

Skill-based service streams bridging talent and devotional contribution. We transform members into confident leaders.

  • Streams: Digital Media (Podcast, Design), Devotional Art (Singing, Painting, Bhajan Clubbing), Event Engagement, and Social Impact Seva.
  • Output-Oriented: Every lab produces real-world deliverables, backed by clear project cycles and dedicated leads.
04

Monthly Youth Masterclass

Professional, transformational workshops addressing real-life youth challenges with spiritual grounding.

  • Accessibility: Open to all (subsidized for members). Paid professional entry format.
  • Experience: Curated high-quality masterclass, fellowship meals, participation certificates, and Srila Prabhupada's books.
05

Inner Beauty Retreat

Quarterly immersive full-day retreats for reflection, identity alignment, and much deeper sisterhood bonding.

  • Highlights: Guided reflection, kirtan meditation, value realignment, and lush nature walks to consolidate spiritual identity.
